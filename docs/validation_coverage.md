# Appendix: trajectory coverage of every channel

Generated by `validation/compare_100_batches.py` together with the [100-batch comparison](validation_100_batches.md).

For each strategy and channel: the number of published samples that lie inside the simulated 5-95 % band at the same time, the number of samples compared, and their share (the coverage). Samples are compared up to the shortest batch of each strategy, published or simulated. When the published and simulated distributions agree, about 90 % of the published samples are expected inside the band, so about 10 % outside is the expected result. The samples of one batch are strongly correlated in time and there are 30 published batches per strategy (10 with faults), so a coverage can differ from 90 % by several percentage points by chance, more for the faulty batches.

**Lowest coverage: 73 %, for `Wt` in the faulty batches.** Over the process states and measurements, the coverage is 80-95 % for the recipe-driven, operator-controlled and APC batches and 73-92 % for the faulty batches, which are the weakest part of the comparison.

Inputs that follow the recipe in both data sets (unless PRBS, PAT control or a fault moves them) lie inside the band almost by construction; they are listed for completeness.
